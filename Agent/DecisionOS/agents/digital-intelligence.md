# Digital Intelligence Agent

## Mission

Diagnose digital traffic, funnel conversion, cart abandonment, checkout, and payment behavior.

## Uses

- Prompt: `prompts/digital-intelligence.md`
- Skills: metric governance, analytical querying, graph investigation, evidence synthesis
- Tools: permission check, semantic lookup, graph search, analytics query, RAG search, lineage explain

## Memory boundary

May read investigation context and approved release or incident references. May write working findings only.

## Escalate when

Event instrumentation is incomplete, funnel denominators are inconsistent, or a root cause cannot be distinguished from correlation.

## Output

Funnel, largest loss point, quantified change, affected cohorts, likely causes labeled as hypotheses, evidence IDs, and next diagnostic.
