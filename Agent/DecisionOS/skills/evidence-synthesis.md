# Evidence Synthesis Skill

## Purpose

Combine graph, analytical, and document evidence into a transparent decision brief.

## Procedure

1. Group evidence by claim.
2. Identify the authoritative source for each claim.
3. Separate observed facts from interpretation and hypothesis.
4. Reconcile conflicting sources or surface the conflict.
5. Assess evidence strength, recency, completeness, and permission scope.
6. State what the evidence does not establish.
7. Form recommendations only after the evidence summary.

## Output labels

Use these labels explicitly:

- Fact
- Interpretation
- Hypothesis
- Recommendation
- Limitation
